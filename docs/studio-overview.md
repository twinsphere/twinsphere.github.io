# twinstudio

twinstudio is the editor of the twinsphere suite. It is a web application for creating, editing and organising the
content of your twinsphere tenant: digital twins, submodels, concept descriptions and files.

Nothing has to be installed. You sign in with your twinsphere ID in a browser.

![twinstudio dashboard](img/twinstudio_general_dashboard.png)

## What you can do with it

- **Create a digital twin** from scratch, by duplicating an existing one, or from a type twin.
- **Fill in its data.** twinstudio guides you through the submodel templates and checks your entries against the
  AAS metamodel while you work.
- **Publish it** to your twinsphere tenant, where the Viewer and the API can use it.
- **Organise your content.** The catalogue lists twins, submodels, concept descriptions, drafts and files, and lets
  you filter, export, duplicate and delete them.
- **Watch your usage.** The statistics page shows how your data has grown over time.
